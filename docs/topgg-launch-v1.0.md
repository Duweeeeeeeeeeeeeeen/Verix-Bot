# Verix - The Ultimate Discord & Next.js Dashboard Suite (Official 1.0 Launch) 🚀

Verix is not just another Discord bot. It is a **premium, all-in-one community management ecosystem** powered by a state-of-the-art Next.js web dashboard. Built for communities, gaming guilds, FiveM servers, and professional organizations, Verix completely eliminates the need for 10 different bots. 

Here is everything Verix does and how it works!

---

## 🎨 Sleek Web Dashboard (Next.js)
Verix brings a state-of-the-art web interface featuring glassmorphic dark-mode aesthetics, real-time sync, and detailed statistics.
*   **How it works:** Simply log in with Discord, select your server, and configure everything visually. No cryptic text commands.
*   **Real-time Concurrency Locks:** If another administrator is editing a module, the page visually locks dynamically to prevent settings conflicts.
*   **Live Analytics:** Track message flows, active users, daily peaking times, and module usage with interactive graphs.

---

## ⚡ 1-Click Setup Wizard
Skip hours of configuration. Verix onboarding gets your server fully operational in under a minute.
*   **How it works:** The setup wizard scans your server structure, lets you toggle modules (Whitelist, Tickets, Verification), select your primary language, and **automatically deploys the necessary Discord channels, categories, and role permissions** for you!

---

## ⚖️ FiveM & Gaming Whitelist System
The most advanced recruitment suite on Discord, perfect for roleplay servers and competitive guilds.
*   **Step 1: Text Application:** Users apply through an interactive web panel or button embed.
*   **Step 2: Automated Review:** Staff can approve or reject applications directly from the dashboard, which automatically notifies the user via DMs.
*   **Step 3: Oral Audition Queue:** Approved applicants join a dedicated voice queue. The bot automatically manages waiting rooms, creates private audition channels, and handles time limits.

---

## 🎫 Premium Ticket & Support System
Keep your community support organized with a premium, high-performance ticket hub.
*   **How it works:** Users click a button embed to open a ticket. Verix assigns a ticket channel, locks down permissions, and notifies your staff.
*   **Staff Claiming:** Staff members can visually "Claim" tickets to take ownership.
*   **Quick Replies:** Pre-configure templates in the dashboard to answer common questions in 1 click.
*   **Interactive Transcripts:** When a ticket closes, Verix automatically generates and saves a beautifully formatted HTML transcript, logging the entire conversation for audit purposes.

---

## 🌍 Universal Multi-Language Ecosystem
Verix is built from the ground up for international communities, fully supporting **English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese**.
*   **How it works:** When you change the bot's primary language, the entire Next.js Web Dashboard, setup channels, command responses, button labels, and system embeds translate **instantly and dynamically**.

---

## 🏆 Engagement & Leveling System
Keep your community active and rewarded.
*   **How it works:** Users gain XP by texting and speaking in voice channels.
*   **Role Rewards:** Automatically unlock specific Discord roles when users reach certain levels.
*   **Rank Cards & Leaderboards:** Users can generate beautiful visual rank cards directly in Discord showing their progression.

---

## 🔒 Advanced Verification & Security
Protect your server from raid bots, spam, and unverified accounts.
*   **How it works:** Create a gateway channel with a verification button. The bot securely validates users and grants them access roles while logging everything to your staff channels.
