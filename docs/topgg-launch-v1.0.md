# Verix - The Ultimate Discord & Next.js Dashboard Suite (Official 1.0 Launch) 🚀

Verix is the only bot you will ever need for your Discord server. It is a **premium, all-in-one community management ecosystem** powered by a state-of-the-art Next.js web dashboard. Specially designed for high-performance servers, gaming guilds, FiveM roleplay servers, and professional creators, Verix consolidates dozens of modular features into a single, beautifully unified experience.

Here is the complete suite of modules included in Verix and how they work!

---

## 🎨 Sleek Web Dashboard (Next.js & Glassmorphism)
Verix brings a state-of-the-art web interface featuring premium dark-mode aesthetics, real-time sync, and detailed statistics.
*   **How it works:** Log in with Discord, select your server, and configure everything visually. No cryptic text commands.
*   **Real-time Concurrency Locks:** Visual locks prevent multiple administrators from modifying the same settings concurrently.
*   **Live Analytics:** Track server activity, message volume, leveling/XP stats, and module engagement in real-time.

---

## ⚡ 1-Click Setup Wizard
Skip hours of configuration. Verix onboarding gets your server fully operational in under a minute.
*   **How it works:** The wizard scans your server structure, lets you toggle modules, select your primary language, and **automatically deploys the necessary Discord channels, categories, and role permissions** for you!

---

## 🌍 Universal Multi-Language Ecosystem
Verix is built from the ground up for international communities, fully supporting **English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese**.
*   **How it works:** Switch languages in the Navbar, Landing Page, Server Selectors, Onboarding Wizard, or Core Settings. The entire Web Dashboard, setup channels, command responses, button labels, and system embeds translate **instantly and dynamically**.

---

## ⚖️ FiveM & Gaming Whitelist System
The most advanced recruitment suite on Discord, perfect for roleplay servers and competitive guilds.
*   **Text Applications:** Users apply through an interactive web panel. Staff can approve or reject applications directly from the dashboard, which automatically notifies the user via DMs.
*   **Oral Audition Queue:** Approved applicants join a dedicated voice queue. The bot automatically manages waiting rooms, creates private audition channels, and handles time limits.
*   **FiveM Status Integrations:** Real-time monitoring of your FiveM servers showing player counts, active players list, and server health.

---

## 🎫 Premium Ticket & Support System
Keep your community support organized with a premium, high-performance ticket hub.
*   **How it works:** Users click a button embed to open a ticket. Verix assigns a ticket channel, locks down permissions, and notifies your staff.
*   **Staff Claiming:** Staff members can visually "Claim" tickets to take ownership.
*   **Quick Replies:** Pre-configure templates in the dashboard to answer common questions in 1 click.
*   **Interactive Transcripts:** When a ticket closes, Verix automatically generates and saves a beautifully formatted HTML transcript, logging the entire conversation for audit purposes.

---

## 📢 Social Alerts (Twitch & YouTube)
Promote your content automatically and never let your followers miss a stream or video.
*   **How it works:** Input your YouTube Channel ID or Twitch Channel name. Verix automatically monitors and sends beautiful, customized embeds to your chosen announcement channels the moment you publish a video or go live.

---

## 🎭 Reaction Roles
Give your members the power to customize their profiles and choose notifications.
*   **How it works:** Create visually stunning custom embeds with reaction button choices. When users click, the bot instantly adds or removes corresponding Discord roles.

---

## 🎉 Giveaway Studio
Automate and run professional, fair giveaways to boost community engagement.
*   **How it works:** Configure prize details, entry requirements (e.g., minimum level or specific roles), durations, and winner counts in the dashboard. The bot handles entries and rolls the winners automatically.

---

## 📸 Photo Contest
Run interactive community contests directly on Discord.
*   **How it works:** Members submit their photos. The bot generates an entry card with vote buttons. Your community votes, and the dashboard tracks the leaderboard automatically!

---

## 📊 Poll Studio
Engage your community and collect quick feedback with live polling.
*   **How it works:** Launch polls with custom choices and time durations. Supports exclusive voting (one choice) or multiple-choice voting, showing live progress bars and voting stats.

---

## 🏆 Engagement & Leveling System
Keep your community active, chatty, and rewarded.
*   **How it works:** Users gain XP by texting and speaking in voice channels.
*   **Role Rewards:** Automatically unlock specific Discord roles when users reach certain levels.
*   **Rank Cards & Leaderboards:** Users can generate beautiful visual rank cards showing their progression, backed by a global web leaderboard.

---

## 🎙️ Temporary Private Voice Rooms
Give your members full control over their voice chats.
*   **How it works:** Set up a "Join to Create" channel. When a user joins, Verix automatically creates a private voice channel, designates them as the owner, and lets them lock, name, or limit the slot size of their room via the dashboard or commands.

---

## 💎 White-Label & Custom Branding (Private Bot)
Customize Verix to make it entirely your own.
*   **How it works:** Enter your private Discord Bot Token in the Whitelabel settings. Verix will run under your custom name, custom avatar, and custom bot status rotation, entirely hiding our default branding!

---

## 🤖 Automations & Scheduler
Run recurring actions automatically without manual intervention.
*   **How it works:** Set up custom scheduler alerts, timed channel lock/unlock states, or cleanups, keeping your server running perfectly on autopilot.

---

## 🛠️ Rich Utility & Purge Tools
Powerful command integrations to manage your chat.
*   **How it works:** Instantly clean up, purge, or bulk-delete messages with advanced filters (e.g., bot messages only, user messages only) directly or from the settings dashboard.

---

## 🛡️ Verification & Security Gateways
Protect your server from raid bots, spam, and unverified accounts.
*   **How it works:** Create a gateway channel with a verification button. The bot securely validates users and grants them access roles while logging everything to your staff channels.

---

## 👥 Collaborators & Permissions
Coordinate safely with your team.
*   **How it works:** Add staff members as server collaborators with restricted permissions, allowing them to manage specific modules without giving them full dashboard control or ownership.
